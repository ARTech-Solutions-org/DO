# Asset production brief

## Use official assets first
Official logo vectors and commercial font files to be supplied separately. Do not generate/AI-reinterpret wordmarks. PDFs in `references/brand/` establish identity, not production-exported vectors. Do NOT copy protected music or portray celebrities/guests not supplied with permission.

## Required custom visual assets (separate image layers)
1. `party-attract-bg` — photographic deep black nightclub with graded violet spotlights, discreet physically plausible disco-ball reflection, no text or logos, portrait full bleed, safe negative space top and bottom.
2. `party-style-bg` — art-directed darker alternate with orange-lavender stage washes and soft film grain; midsection contrast for cards, no lettering.
3. `camera-frame` — clean transparent *overlay* with understated chromatic club light corners; transparent center for live video, not simulated photo/people.
4. `countdown-flare` — additive light/shutter flash animation, transparent visual layer or CSS/SVG; no fake literal clock.
5. `processing-loop` — physical spinning vinyl/disco light movement or subtle layered spotlight sequence; actual animation from CSS/GSAP/3D, not pre-rendered whole UI.
6. `result-frame` — print-friendly branded transparent frame/background with logo lockup allocated approved clear space; must not obscure face, adaptable to multiple output ratios.
7. `thank-you-bg` — subtle luminous club finale with strong headline-safe negative space, optionally reusable attract background.
8. `style-caricature-example` — clear *illustration of style* made with a consenting model or fictional face; sample only, not a guest output.
9. `optional chrome motifs` — transparent metallic disc/disco ball art, hand-tuned CSS/SVG shadows rather than compositing fake UI.

## Image-generation prompts (one artifact per output, never text/logo baked in)
**Attract:** Premium real-world nightclub stage light photography, elegant deep purple tungsten-blue and subtle amber beams on a black dance floor, mirror disco ball in upper third, natural lens bloom and realistic grain, luxury music event editorial art direction, minimalist large negative space central area for digital typography, no people in foreground, no branding, no text, portrait 9:16, photoreal materials, avoid CGI glossy blobs.

**Style:** Sophisticated night club poster background, deep charcoal shadows with a single warm tangerine pool of light and violet wash, faint physical stage haze, textured analogue photography, subtle horizontal spotlight sweeps, negative space for multiple selectable cards, no signs, no lettering, no logos, portrait 9:16.

**Result:** Realist dark evening party photo-frame background, very restrained violet-orange reflected lights, elegant analog-film grain, warm nightclub ambience, neutral blank center for a portrait inset, no text, no logos, portrait 9:16.

**Thank You:** Moody live music venue architecture with restrained purple spotlight silhouettes and one warm accent beam, editorial photograph, airy negative space behind center headline, no people, no text, no brand, portrait 9:16.

**Caricature sample:** A friendly adult fictional guest's shoulder-up caricature with intentional proportional exaggeration, polished professional illustrator's ink and painterly shading, expressive but recognizable eyes/smile, realistic believable skin tones, understated violet and tangerine editorial nightlife background, no text/logo/watermark, portrait composition.

## Asset QC
- A generated reference image may be imperfect, distorted, and not pixel accurate. Iterate and art-direct until approved; use real fonts and brand assets in UI.
- Separately store optimized AVIF/WebP for kiosk and high-res PNG source files; no unlicensed fonts.
- Carefully check faces, fingers, reflections, pixel dimensions, backgrounds and colors before accepting.
